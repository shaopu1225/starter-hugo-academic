I’m Shaopu Song (宋少朴). I am working on LLM training infra @MiniMax. Before joining MiniMax, I was a team member of AML @Bytedance, working on optimizing training framework for all Recommendation/Ads/Search business in the entire Bytedance ecosystem, such as Douyin, TikTok, shop, and live, etc.

My main interests are distributed systems. Before working on Machine Learning System for LLM, I mainly worked on building distributed storage system, and engaged in building the prototype of [Skystore](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.20818v1), a global object store with S3 interface that optimize performance and cost @Sky Computing Lab. Also, I have 4-year experience on building intelligence robotic systems during my undergraduate period.
